## What Is a DSCR Loan for a Short-Term Rental?

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio — the measure of whether a property's rental income covers its debt payments. A [DSCR loan](/dscr-loans) is a rental-property mortgage that qualifies you based on that ratio instead of your personal income.

The math is simple. Lenders divide the property's monthly rental income by its total monthly debt obligation — principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and any HOA dues, together known as PITIA.

**DSCR = Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly PITIA**

A DSCR of 1.25 means the property earns 25% more than it costs to carry. A DSCR of 1.0 means it breaks even. Most lenders in 2026 want to see a ratio of at least 1.0 to 1.25, though some programs — including ours — will work with ratios below 1.0 when the deal and the borrower are strong.

The reason this matters for Airbnb investors is that short-term rental income is usually much higher than long-term rent for the same property. A condo that leases for $2,800 a month might gross $6,000 as a well-run vacation rental. That higher income produces a stronger DSCR, which can unlock more leverage and better pricing — if the lender knows how to underwrite it. Learning [how to calculate DSCR](/blog/how-to-calculate-dscr) before you apply lets you run the numbers on a target property yourself.

## How Lenders Calculate Short-Term Rental Income

This is where STR financing gets its own rulebook. Because Airbnb income swings with seasons and occupancy, lenders don't simply take a peak-month figure and run with it. They use one of three documentation methods, then apply a discount.

### The Three Income Documentation Methods

For a property with an operating history, the cleanest path is a **trailing 12-month income statement** — actual booking revenue from your Airbnb or VRBO dashboard, which proves real performance across a full season cycle.

For a property without a track record, lenders order an **AirDNA or STR market report** that projects gross revenue from comparable listings by location, bedroom count, and amenity tier. A third option is a **1007 or 1025 rent schedule** from the appraiser, adjusted for short-term rents.

### The STR Haircut

Whatever the source, expect the lender to apply a discount — often called a haircut — of roughly 10% to 25% to the gross figure. In practice, most programs qualify a property on 70% to 80% of projected short-term rental income to account for vacancy, seasonality, and cleaning and management costs.

| Documentation Method | Best For | Typical Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Trailing 12-month statement | Properties with operating history | Lender uses actual net-of-seasonality income |
| AirDNA / STR market report | New or recently converted STRs | 70%–80% of projected gross applied |
| 1007 / 1025 rent schedule | Standard appraisal path | Adjusted to STR comps, then discounted |

The takeaway: the stronger your documentation, the higher the income the lender will credit. A property with twelve clean months of booking history almost always qualifies for more than one relying on projections alone.

### Confirm Your STR Is Actually Legal

Before ordering an appraisal, verify that short-term rentals are permitted at the **city, county, and HOA level**. Miami Beach, for example, allows STRs only in specific zones and requires a registered permit. A property that can't legally operate as an Airbnb can't be underwritten as one — this is the single most common reason STR deals fall apart, and it's entirely avoidable with a phone call to the local zoning office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you get a DSCR loan for an Airbnb or short-term rental?**

Yes. DSCR loans are one of the most popular ways to finance short-term rentals because they qualify on the property's projected rental income rather than your personal tax returns. Lenders use a trailing 12-month income statement, an AirDNA market report, or an adjusted appraisal rent schedule to establish income, then apply a discount for seasonality. A property with strong short-term rental numbers can often carry more leverage than it would as a long-term rental.

**How do lenders calculate short-term rental income for a DSCR loan?**

Lenders start with gross short-term rental income — from your actual booking history, an AirDNA projection, or an appraiser's rent schedule — then apply a haircut of roughly 10% to 25% to account for vacancy and seasonality. Most programs qualify the property on 70% to 80% of projected gross income. The stronger your documentation, especially twelve months of real booking history, the higher the income the lender will credit.

Expect a minimum DSCR of 1.0 to 1.25, a down payment of 20% to 25%, a credit score of at least 620 (680+ for the best pricing), and six to twelve months of PITIA in reserves. You'll also need to confirm the property is legally permitted for short-term rental use.

**Do I need short-term rental permits to qualify?**
