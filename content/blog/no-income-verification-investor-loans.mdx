## What Is a No Income Verification Investor Loan?

A no income verification investor loan (sometimes called a "no-doc loan" or "stated income loan") is a real estate loan where the lender does not require W-2s, pay stubs, or personal tax returns to approve the loan.

Instead of income, lenders evaluate:

- **The property's value** — as-is and after repair (for fix-and-flip)
- **The property's cash flow** — for rental and DSCR loans
- **Loan-to-value or loan-to-cost ratios**
- **The borrower's exit strategy** — sale, refinance, or hold

These loans are primarily used by real estate investors, not owner-occupants. Federal lending rules require income verification for owner-occupied homes. Investment properties operate under different guidelines, which is what makes no-doc investor loans legal and widely available in the private lending market.

## Who Qualifies for a No Income Verification Investor Loan?

### Self-Employed Investors

Self-employed borrowers are the most common users of no-income-verification loans. If you own a business, your personal tax return often understates your actual cash position — deductions, depreciation, and business expenses reduce your reported income below what you actually earn.

Conventional lenders penalize this. Private lenders don't. At Funded Capital, we evaluate the deal and your track record as an investor — not what your Schedule C shows.

### LLC and Entity Borrowers

Many experienced investors hold properties in LLCs or other entities for liability protection. Most banks will not lend to LLCs without a personal guarantee, full entity documentation, and months of underwriting. Private lenders typically accommodate entity borrowing and can close in the entity's name without requiring you to strip out personal income documentation.

See our full guide to [LLC real estate loans](/blog/llc-real-estate-loan) if you're structuring your portfolio through an entity.

### Foreign Nationals

Foreign national investors face near-impossible documentation hurdles at conventional banks — ITIN-only borrowers, limited U.S. credit history, and income earned abroad all become disqualifying factors. No income verification investor loans remove these barriers. Funded Capital lends to foreign nationals across our [fix-and-flip](/fix-and-flip-loans), [DSCR](/dscr-loans), and [new construction](/new-construction-loans) programs.

### Buy-and-Hold Investors Using DSCR

For rental property investors, Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loans are the most common form of no-income-verification financing. Instead of your personal income, the lender looks at whether the property's rental income covers the mortgage payment.

A DSCR above 1.0 means the property generates more rent than it costs to service the loan. Most lenders want a DSCR of 1.1–1.25 or higher. Funded Capital offers [DSCR loans](/dscr-loans) starting at 6.0% with up to 80% LTV — and no W-2 or tax return required.

### First-Time Investors

First-time investors with strong deal fundamentals can also qualify. If the property has a clear path to profitability and the exit strategy is well-defined, Funded Capital will underwrite first-time borrowers on [fix-and-flip loans](/fix-and-flip-loans) and DSCR loans.

## What Lenders Actually Look At Instead of Income

When a lender says "no income verification," they don't mean no underwriting. They mean the underwriting focuses on the asset and the deal — not your personal financial situation. Here's what actually matters:

| Factor | Fix & Flip | DSCR Rental | New Construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Credit score | 620+ typical | 640+ typical | 640+ typical |
| Loan-to-cost (LTC) | Up to 90% | N/A | Up to 85% |
| Loan-to-value (LTV) | N/A | Up to 80% | N/A |
| Property income | Not required | DSCR ≥ 1.0 | Not required |
| Experience required | Preferred | Preferred | Preferred |
| Entity borrowing | Yes | Yes | Yes |

### Credit Score

Even without income verification, most private lenders require a minimum credit score — typically 620 to 680. Your credit score signals how you manage debt obligations, which matters even when income isn't the primary qualifier. At Funded Capital, we work with investors across the credit spectrum, including those rebuilding credit after a past event.

### The Deal Itself

For fix-and-flip loans, the key numbers are the purchase price, estimated renovation cost, and after-repair value (ARV). We lend up to 90% of the total project cost (LTC) on fix-and-flip loans, starting at 8.75%.

For DSCR loans, we evaluate the property's gross rental income against the proposed loan payment. Use our [loan calculator](/calculator) to stress-test your DSCR before applying.

### Exit Strategy

Every no-income-verification investor loan needs a clear exit. For short-term loans (fix-and-flip, new construction), the exit is typically a sale or cash-out refinance. For DSCR loans, the exit is long-term hold with rental income sustaining the debt service. Lenders want to see that you've thought through what happens at maturity.

### Reserves

Some programs require borrowers to show liquid reserves — typically 3–6 months of loan payments — to demonstrate that they can service the debt even if the property sits vacant or the renovation takes longer than expected.
