## What Is a Hard Money Loan?

A hard money loan is a short-term, asset-based loan from a private lender secured by real estate. Unlike conventional bank loans, hard money lenders qualify borrowers based on the property's value and deal economics — not W-2s, tax returns, or employment history.

For Florida investors, hard money loans offer three advantages that banks simply can't match:

1. **Speed**: Term sheets in hours, closings in days — not weeks or months.
2. **Flexibility**: No income verification on most programs; LLC borrowing encouraged.
3. **Deal-focused underwriting**: The lender evaluates the property and exit strategy, not your employment history.

## What Florida Hard Money Lenders Look For

When you apply for a hard money loan in Florida, lenders evaluate these four factors:

### Property Value and Deal Economics

The property is the primary collateral. Lenders analyze the as-is value, after-repair value (ARV), loan-to-cost ratio (LTC), and your exit strategy — sale or refinance. A strong deal with a clear exit can overcome a weaker borrower profile.

### Credit Score

Most Florida hard money lenders prefer a 680+ credit score. However, credit is less important than the deal itself. Strong ARV, experienced borrower, and solid exit strategy carry more weight than a perfect FICO score.

### Experience

Experienced investors typically get better rates and higher leverage. First-time borrowers can still qualify — especially when working with a licensed general contractor on a fix-and-flip or when the DSCR on a rental is comfortably above 1.0x.

### Equity / Down Payment

Expect to put in 10–20% of the total project cost. The more skin in the game, the better the terms you'll receive.

## How to Choose the Right Hard Money Lender in Florida

Not every private lender is worth your time. Here's what to look for:

**Florida licensing.** Confirm the lender is licensed to originate loans in Florida. Ask for their NMLS number and verify at nmlsconsumeraccess.org.

**In-house underwriting.** Lenders that underwrite in-house move faster and have more control over the process. Avoid lenders who outsource underwriting — they add days or weeks to your timeline.

**Transparent fee disclosure.** Ask for a full breakdown of all fees upfront: origination points, appraisal fees, draw fees, extension fees, and prepayment penalties. A reliable lender will hand this over without hesitation.

**Track record in your market.** A lender based in Florida will understand local appraisal timelines, title company relationships, and market-specific ARV data better than one operating from across the country.

**Speed guarantee.** Ask specifically: "How fast can you issue a term sheet, and what is your average time to close?" Then hold them to it.
