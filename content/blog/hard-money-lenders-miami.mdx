## What Is a Hard Money Lender?

A hard money lender is a private lender — an individual or company — that provides short-term, asset-based loans secured by real estate. Unlike banks, hard money lenders focus on the value of the property and the deal economics rather than your W-2, tax returns, or employment history.

For Miami real estate investors, this distinction matters. Many of the best deals in this market are won by investors who can move quickly and close without the income verification hurdles that slow down conventional lenders.

**Key characteristics of hard money loans:**

- Loan approval based on the property's value, not your personal income
- Term sheets issued in hours, not weeks
- Closings in as little as 5–14 business days
- Short loan terms (typically 12–24 months)
- Higher interest rates than conventional loans — offset by speed and flexibility

## What Miami Hard Money Lenders Look For

When you apply for a hard money loan in Miami, lenders are primarily evaluating four things:

### 1. The Property and Deal Economics

The property is the primary collateral. Lenders will evaluate:

- **As-is value**: What is the property worth today?
- **After-repair value (ARV)**: What will it be worth after renovation?
- **Loan-to-cost (LTC)**: What percentage of the total project cost are you borrowing?
- **Exit strategy**: How do you plan to repay the loan — sale or refinance?

Use our free [loan calculator](/calculator) to run your ARV and DSCR numbers before you apply.

### 2. Your Experience

Most lenders prefer borrowers with at least some real estate investment experience, but many — including Funded Capital — will work with first-time investors when the deal economics are strong and the exit strategy is clear.

### 3. Credit Score

Hard money lenders are more flexible than banks, but credit still matters. Most lenders in Miami prefer a 680+ credit score, though strong deals can sometimes offset a lower score.

### 4. Skin in the Game

Lenders want to see that you have equity in the deal. Most hard money loans require a down payment of 10–20% of the total project cost.

## How to Choose a Hard Money Lender in Miami

Not all hard money lenders are equal. Here's what separates a reliable lender from one that will cost you a deal — or worse, your deposit.

**Look for local market knowledge.** A lender based in Miami understands neighborhood dynamics, permit timelines, contractor availability, and ARV ranges in a way that an out-of-state lender simply doesn't.

**Demand a term sheet upfront.** Any reputable lender should be able to issue a preliminary term sheet within 24–48 hours of receiving your application. If a lender is vague about rates or terms early in the process, that's a red flag.

**Ask about their closing track record.** Speed matters in Miami. Ask how quickly they've closed comparable loans and whether they have in-house underwriting or rely on third parties.

**Check for hidden fees.** Extension fees, draw fees, inspection fees, and prepayment penalties can add up. Ask for a full fee disclosure before you proceed.

**Verify they fund in Florida.** Not every hard money lender is licensed to lend in Florida. Confirm licensing before investing time in the application process.
