## 3. Voice Principles

*Note on the examples below: "term sheet in 2 hours" was confirmed by Luis on 2026-09-25 as our average. Say it as an average, never as a promise.*

### 3.1 Speak Investor-to-Investor
Write like you're a successful investor talking to another investor — not a corporation talking to a customer. You've been in the room. You know what it feels like to lose a deal because a lender moved slow.

**✅ Do:** "When timing is everything, 2 hours matters."
**❌ Don't:** "We are committed to providing expedited financing solutions for our valued clients."

### 3.2 Lead with the Number or the Fact
Every claim needs proof. Never make an abstract statement when you can make a specific one.

**✅ Do:** "Term sheet in 2 hours. Close in 5–10 business days."
**❌ Don't:** "We move fast to help you close deals quickly."

### 3.3 Be Confident, Not Corporate
We don't hedge. We don't say "we strive to" or "our goal is to." We state what we do.

**✅ Do:** "No hidden fees. No bait-and-switch."
**❌ Don't:** "We aim to provide transparent pricing and strive to avoid surprises at closing."

### 3.4 Short Sentences Win
If you can cut a word, cut it. If a sentence has two ideas, split it into two sentences.

**✅ Do:** "Apply in minutes. Term sheet in 2 hours. Close in 5–10 business days."
**❌ Don't:** "Our streamlined application process allows investors to quickly submit their loan request and receive a preliminary term sheet within approximately two business hours."

### 3.5 Miami Energy
We're based in Miami. We're fast, direct, and a little bold. Not flashy — bold. There's confidence in our copy without arrogance. We celebrate the win with our clients ("You close the deal. We celebrate with you.").

## 4. Hard Copy Rules — NON-NEGOTIABLE

These apply to EVERY piece of content Claude generates for Funded Capital.

| Rule | ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines: 7 words max | "Close in 5–10 Business Days" | "Funded Capital Provides Fast and Reliable Financing for Real Estate Investors" |
| No filler openers | Start with the hook | Never start with "At Funded Capital, we believe…" |
| Body copy: 2 sentences max per section in emails | — | — |
| Email total body copy: 120 words max | — | — |
| Every section ends in a CTA or a fact | — | Never end a section with a vague close |
| Numbers beat adjectives | "Fix & Flip from 8.75%" | "competitive rates", or any rate written as a promise |
| Active voice only | "We close in 5–10 business days" | "Closings can be completed in as few as five business days" |
| Banned words | — | committed to, dedicated to, passionate about, seamless, synergy, robust, solution, leverage (as a verb), utilize |
| Closing speed: always the range, never the floor | "Close in 5–10 business days, deal-dependent" | "Close in 5 days" or "in as little as 5 days" — 5 is the floor, not the typical case |
| State coverage: 45 states | "45 states — excluded: VT, UT, OR, SD, ND" | "44 states" or an unqualified "all 50 states" |
| Audience: investors only | "Built for real estate investors" | Anything addressed to a homebuyer, homeowner, or primary residence |
| Rates: starting points, stated as ranges | "Fix & Flip from 8.75%" · "DSCR from 6.0%" | Any rate presented as a guarantee, or a single rate with no "from" |
| Credit: tiers, not a floor | "Most programs 660+, best pricing 680+, 640–659 case by case" | "680 minimum" — there is no hard 680 floor |
| Ground-up leverage | See §4.1 — never a flat "85% LTC" | "85% LTC" full stop |
| Compliance line | Once per piece that makes a lending claim, in the caption or the email body | The line set on a slide or burned into an image |
| Numbers in pricing content | Pulled from the pricing engine, cited with a source and a date | Any AI-generated number in borrower-facing content that prices a deal |

### 4.1 Ground-up construction leverage — say it exactly this way

Never write a flat "85% LTC" for ground-up. The structure has three parts:

- **Standard leverage: 85% of full cost** — purchase price + sunk costs + the remaining construction budget.
- **Tier 5 builders: 90% of full cost.** Tier 5 means **five or more completed ground-up projects.**
- **Interest reserve: 5% of cost on top of either number.** That is how a Tier 5 builder reaches **95% all-in**. The reserve band finances the interest reserve only — **it never buys construction dollars.**
- **After-repair LTV is a second, separate cap. The lower of the two governs.**

*(This was wrong in the pricing engine until it was fixed on 2026-09-23. Tier 5 builders were being quoted five points too low. Anything written before that date that quotes ground-up leverage should be re-checked.)*

### 4.2 The compliance line

Include this once — exactly once — in anything that makes a lending claim:

> Terms are subject to underwriting, appraisal, title, and insurance.

It belongs in the **caption or the email body**. It never goes on a slide, in a carousel image, or burned into a graphic.

### 4.3 Numbers rule

**No AI-generated numbers in anything borrower-facing that prices a deal.** Pricing is deterministic code — rates, leverage, payments and term sheets come from the pricing engine, not from a model's estimate. Every published rate or market claim cites its **source and its date**.

## 5. Email Newsletter Rules

### Structure (every email follows this)
```
[Header image or stat callout — visual, not text-heavy]
[Subject line: 6 words max, no clickbait]
[Hook line: 1 sentence, leads with a number or bold claim]
[Body: 2–3 short paragraphs, max 40 words each]
[Single CTA button — action + time: "Apply Now — 2 Min"]
[Footer: minimal — logo, phone, unsubscribe]
```

### Subject Line Formula
Use one of these patterns:
- **Number + outcome:** "Close in 5–10 Business Days — Here's How"
- **Question that stings:** "Why Are Investors Still Using Slow Lenders?"
- **Bold statement:** "Banks Said No. We Said Yes."
- **Urgency + benefit:** "New Rates Live — DSCR From 6.0%"

### What Kiavi Does That We Should Copy
- Lead with one big data stat in the hero — *"$500M+ funded" and "1,200+ deals closed" were confirmed by Luis on 2026-09-25*
- Icon-based feature sections instead of paragraphs
- Short CTAs with urgency ("See Your Rate")
- Mobile-first layout — one column, big tap targets

### What to STOP Doing
- ❌ 4+ paragraph explanations of loan products
- ❌ Multiple CTAs competing for attention
- ❌ Introductory filler ("We hope this email finds you well...")
- ❌ Bullet lists with 6+ items
- ❌ Recapping what the company does in every email

*Why: Specificity beats category claim. Every lender says "fast." We name the number — and we name the range, not the floor. ("Term sheet in 2 hours" was confirmed by Luis on 2026-09-25 as an average.)*

**Before:** "We are committed to providing transparent terms with no hidden fees, ensuring that investors can trust what they see in the term sheet is what they receive at closing."
**After:** "No hidden fees. No bait-and-switch. What you see in the term sheet is what you get at closing."
*Why: Same meaning. 63% fewer words. More credible because it's direct.*

### Email Opening
**Before:** "We hope this message finds you well. As real estate investors continue to navigate a challenging market, Funded Capital remains dedicated to providing the fast, flexible financing solutions you need to succeed."
**After:** "DSCR from 6.0% — and we close in 5–10 business days."
*Why: Leads with value immediately. The reader knows in 2 seconds why to keep reading. Note "from", not "at": starting rates are ranges, never guarantees. A rate-movement claim like "rates just dropped" needs a source and a date.*

**Before:** "We invite you to learn more about our broker program and the many benefits of partnering with Funded Capital for your clients' financing needs."
**After:** "Earn up to 3% per closed loan. Apply takes 2 minutes."
*Why: Answers "what's in it for me" immediately with a specific number. The broker fee range of 0.5%–3% per closed loan was confirmed by Luis on 2026-09-25; "up to 3%" is the ceiling, not the typical fee.*
